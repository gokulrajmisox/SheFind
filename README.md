## 📖 Project Context

Despite the availability of numerous scholarships and government schemes designed specifically to empower women and girl students, many remain completely unaware of these opportunities. Currently, critical information is heavily fragmented—scattered across dozens of disconnected government websites, distinct scholarship portals, departmental pages, and independent organization websites.

This decentralization creates significant barriers, making it difficult for students to:
- 🔍 **Find relevant opportunities** suited to their specific profiles.
- 📋 **Understand eligibility criteria** without sifting through complex legal jargon.
- ⚖️ **Compare benefits** across different schemes to maximize financial aid.
- ⏰ **Track application deadlines** to avoid missing out.
- 🏛️ **Identify the correct official application portal** and avoid third-party hurdles.
- 🔖 **Remember saved opportunities** for future reference.

**Our Solution:** SheFind is designed to bridge this information gap. We bring these scattered opportunities together into a single, unified, searchable, and highly organized platform.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Discover Opportunities:** Browse an extensive, curated directory of scholarships and government schemes dedicated to women.
- **Smart Search:** Quickly locate specific programs, departments, or opportunities using keywords.
- **Category Filtering:** Filter schemes by specific demographic or focus categories (e.g., STEM, Minority, Single Girl Child).
- **Education Level Filtering:** Narrow down programs based on the applicant's current educational pursuit (e.g., High School, Undergraduate, Postgraduate, Ph.D.).
